# Review

Read [review-standards.md](../review-standards.md) and [reproducibility-checklist.md](../reproducibility-checklist.md). Use the paper's type and the available source coverage to scope all judgments. Follow supplied venue criteria if available; otherwise describe this as a general critical assessment.

## 1. Identity, scope, and summary

Identify the paper and reviewed material. Summarize its problem, claimed contribution, approach, and central evidence in a short paragraph, then give an evidence-based overall assessment. Do not substitute a rating for the summary.

## 2. Strengths

List substantive strengths with specific evidence locations. Include only strengths supported by the material; no mandatory count.

## 3. Weaknesses

Separate major issues that change the main conclusion from minor issues affecting clarity or bounded completeness. For each substantive issue provide:

- Location and the specific claim/behavior at issue.
- Evidence for the concern and why it affects the claim.
- A proportionate clarification, analysis, or experiment that could resolve it.

Do not treat unavailable material as proof of author omission. State scope limits where relevant.

## 4. Claim–evidence mapping

| Author claim | Source and scope | Evidence: experiment / proof / observation | Assessment | Gap or boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Populate the central claims using the standards' evidence rubric. Preserve distinctions between unsupported, contradicted, and unknown.

## 5. Reproduction / verification readiness

Provide the checklist's applicable status table, material-access limits, and key obstacles. Keep this separate from the overall scientific assessment; missing training details are N/A for a purely analytic proof.

## 6. Questions for authors

Ask specific, answerable questions whose answers could change the assessment. Check that they are not already answered in accessible text or supplements. No fixed question quota.

## 7. Recommendation and confidence, when useful

Give an accept/reject recommendation or numeric rating only when requested or when the user supplies a venue rubric requiring it. Explain the basis. Report confidence from material coverage, checks performed, and unresolved uncertainties; never claim professional expertise as a credential.

User formatting constraints may compress these elements into prose or bullets. Diagram defaults follow the [entrypoint](../../SKILL.md).
